HEIC to JPG

Turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPG or PNG.

No server receives the photo file: it is opened and converted inside this browser tab.

How to use it

  1. Choose your HEIC photos

    Choose up to 50 HEIC photos with the box above, or drop them on it.

  2. Pick the settings

    Choose JPG or PNG. Leave “Remove location” on if you plan to share the photos. Turn on “Remove all camera and date info” to remove the camera and date info (EXIF). The color profile stays.

  3. Download

    Download each photo, or take them all in one ZIP.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/heic-to-jpg/.
