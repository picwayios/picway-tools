GIF to MP4

Turn an animated GIF into an MP4 video.

The GIF is read and turned into a video by your browser, inside this page. This page has no server that receives your GIF.

How to use it

  1. Choose the GIF

    Tap the box above and pick an animated GIF. The page shows its size in pixels, how many frames it has, how long it runs and how big the file is.

  2. Pick the settings

    Choose a size, how many times in a row the animation plays (once, or 2, 3, 5 or 10 times) and whether transparent areas are filled with white or black.

  3. Convert and download

    Press Convert to MP4 and keep the tab open. The page shows the video, then offers it to download.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/gif-to-mp4/.
