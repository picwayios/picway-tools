PicWay Tools

Compress video

Make a video smaller to send or share.

The video is read and re-encoded by your browser, inside this page. This page has no server that receives your video.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video

    Tap the box above and pick a MOV or MP4 from your iPhone or camera. Once it reads the file, the page shows its size, resolution, frame rate and format.

  2. Pick the settings

    Balanced keeps the picture sharp. Smallest suits chat and email. Choose the 1080p option (long side up to 1920 pixels) or the 720p option (up to 1280 pixels) to shrink it further.

  3. Compress and download

    Press Compress and keep the tab open until it finishes. Then download the MP4.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/compress-video/.
