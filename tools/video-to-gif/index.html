Video to GIF

Turn part of a video into an animated GIF.

The video is read and turned into a GIF by your browser, inside this page. This page has no server that receives your video.

How to use it

  1. Choose the video

    Tap the box above and pick an MP4 or MOV from your iPhone, camera or a screen recording. The page shows its size, resolution, frame rate, length and video format.

  2. Pick the clip and settings

    Type the second where the GIF should start and choose a length: 2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15 or 20 seconds. Then pick a frame rate, a width and whether the GIF loops.

  3. Make and download

    Press Make GIF and keep the tab open. The page shows a preview of the GIF, then offers it to download.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/video-to-gif/.
