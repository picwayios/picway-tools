PicWay Tools

Remove photo location

Take the GPS location out of a photo before you share it.

The photo is read and cleaned by your browser, inside this page. This page has no server that receives your photo.

How to use it

  1. Choose your photos

    Tap the box above and pick JPG or HEIC photos, or drop them on it. The page first lists what each photo contains.

  2. Choose what to remove

    “Remove location” is on by default. Turn on “Remove everything” to also drop camera, lens and date info.

  3. Check and download

    Each new file is read again to confirm the location is gone. Then download it, or take everything in one ZIP.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/remove-photo-location/.
