PicWay Tools

Photo border

Add a border, or make a photo square without cropping.

The photo is read and drawn inside this page, in your browser. This page has no server that receives your photo.

How to use it

  1. Choose a photo

    Tap the box above and pick a JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photo.

  2. Pick a style

    Choose an even border, a Polaroid card with room for a caption, or padding that makes the photo 1:1 or 4:5 without cropping it.

  3. Set the width and color, then download

    Slide the width, pick a color, and save the result as JPG or PNG.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/photo-border/.
