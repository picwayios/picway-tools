PicWay Tools

Photo collage

Put several photos into one image or one long strip.

The photos are read and drawn inside this page in your browser. This page has no server that receives your photos.

How to use it

  1. Add your photos

    Tap the box above and pick your photos or screenshots. HEIC photos work too.

  2. Choose a layout

    Pick Collage for a grid in a 1:1, 4:5, 3:4 or 9:16 shape, or Long strip to stack photos from top to bottom. Use the arrows to change the order.

  3. Download

    Check the preview, then download the result as JPG or PNG.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/photo-collage/.
