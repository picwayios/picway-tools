MOV to MP4

Turn a MOV video into an MP4.

The video is read and converted by your browser, inside this page. This page has no server that receives your video.

How to use it

  1. Choose the MOV

    Tap the box above and pick a MOV from your iPhone, camera or a screen recording. The page shows its size, resolution, frame rate and video format.

  2. Check the video setting

    An H.264 video is copied as it is. For an HEVC video, keep “Re-encode to H.264” so more devices and apps can play it (on iPhone and iPad, the page won’t start it if the new file is estimated to be over 300 MB), or choose “Copy the video as it is” for a fast copy.

  3. Convert and download

    Press Convert to MP4 and keep the tab open. The page checks the new file, then offers the MP4 to download.

This tool is also online at picway.app/tools/mov-to-mp4/.
