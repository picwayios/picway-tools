## Description

```text
PicWay Photo Editor puts five PicWay tools in one tab. Your files are processed inside your browser, and this extension has no server that receives them. The extension asks for no browser permissions.

What's inside

Crop image
Crop, rotate or flip a photo. Keep any shape or pick an aspect ratio such as the original, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 or 16:9, or type the size in pixels.

Resize images
Change the width and height in pixels or percent, up to 50 files in the list, with the aspect ratio kept if you like.

Blur image
Pixelate, blur or black out part of a photo. Drag across the part you want to hide, or apply it to the whole image.

Photo border
Add a border, or make a photo square without cropping. Choose an even border, a card-style frame, or pad the photo to 1:1 or 4:5.

Photo collage
Put several photos into one image or one long strip: up to 20 photos in a collage, or up to 30 in a long strip from top to bottom.

How to use
1. Click the extension's button in the toolbar. A tab opens with the tools.
2. Pick a tool, choose your files, and change the settings if you like.
3. Download the result.

The same tools are also on the web:
Crop image: https://picway.app/tools/crop-image/
Resize images: https://picway.app/tools/resize-image/
Blur image: https://picway.app/tools/blur-image/
Photo border: https://picway.app/tools/photo-border/
Photo collage: https://picway.app/tools/photo-collage/

About PicWay
PicWay is an iPhone app for sorting your photos. Swipe left or right to decide which photos to keep. Private Vault is a separate place for the photos, videos and files you want to keep apart: you open it by drawing a pattern, and you reach it by long-pressing the search button in the tab bar. PicWay also edits videos (cut out a section in the middle, or change the speed of parts of a clip), adds custom filters, converts formats, cleans up similar and duplicate photos, and searches your library. You'll also find memories from this day in past years, collages, compression, and Life Photos Only, which puts screenshots, screen recordings and animated images aside. PicWay browses, analyzes, compresses, organizes, and deletes your photos and videos on your iPhone. PicWay has no server that receives your photos. Your photos and videos leave this phone in only one case: the Private Vault’s iCloud sync (on by default). Items are encrypted and stored in your own iCloud, and only you can open them. PicWay isn't on the App Store yet. Learn more at https://picway.app/
```
